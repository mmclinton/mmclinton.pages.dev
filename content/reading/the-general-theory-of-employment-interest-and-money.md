## Review

Keynes has managed the remarkable feat of dressing up age-old inflationary fallacies in a fog of elaborate algebra and novel terminology, convincing a generation of economists that the oldest of errors, that spending is wealth and debt is prosperity, is in fact a profound scientific discovery.

It will remain a mystery to me why the Keynesian disciples defend such capricious epistemological foundations.

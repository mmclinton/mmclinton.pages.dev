How this site is built.

- **Generator:** [Zola](https://www.getzola.org/) — a single static binary.
- **Type:** [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/), self-hosted and subset.
- **Palette:** the GNOME/Adwaita colors.
- **Hosting:** Cloudflare Pages, deployed from my own Forgejo.
- **JavaScript:** a theme toggle and one small constellation canvas. That's it.

No trackers, no analytics, no cookies.

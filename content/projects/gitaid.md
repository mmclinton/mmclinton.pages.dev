A small CLI that wraps the handful of git operations I reach for most — interactive
fixups, safe force-pushes, and quick branch cleanup — behind commands I can actually
remember.

## Why

The porcelain is powerful but the muscle memory is brutal. `gitaid` trades a few
flags for verbs.
